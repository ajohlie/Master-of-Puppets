## 5. SEO Metadata

- **Title tag:** `NEXUS3` — **6 characters**. Far too short; doesn't describe what the company does or include any searchable keywords. This is the single biggest metadata gap on the site.
- **Meta description:** "Nexus3 builds and operates AI-native companies. Full stack — the software, the operations, and the business." — **108 characters**, well under the 140–160 ideal. Reads well but is leaving useful space on the table.
- **OG tags:** og:title and og:description match the title/meta description above (og:type: website, og:url present). **og:image is missing entirely** — link shares to Slack/X/LinkedIn/iMessage will show no preview image.
- **H1 headings: 9 found** — a real structural issue. A page should have exactly one H1; here nearly every section heading ("INNOVATE.ACCELERATE.BUILD.", "HOW WE BUILD COMPANIES", "THE PLAYBOOK", "NEXUS3 OPERATING COMPANIES", "NX3 LABS", "NEXUS3 CAPITAL", "JOIN US", etc.) is marked as H1 rather than using a proper H1→H2→H3 hierarchy.
- **H2 (11):** NEXUS3 · the full tagline sentence · THE NEXUS3 PLATFORM · A LETTER FROM TIM & JOHN STOJKA · WE'RE BUILDING THE COMPANIES OF THE FUTURE · LET'S BUILD SOMETHING · THREE PEOPLE. ONE THESIS. · WE BUILD THE COMPANIES. WE RUN THEM. · MOST AI COMPANIES ARE BUILDING WRAPPERS... · WE LOOK FOR COMPANIES WHERE AI ISN'T A FEATURE... · OUR INVESTMENTS
- **H3 (31 total, notable ones):** → HOW WE BUILD, → NX3 PLAYBOOK, → INDUSTRIES WE LIKE, → VITAMIN TO PAINKILLER, NEXUS3 OPERATING COMPANIES, NX3 LABS, NEXUS3 CAPITAL, DOMAIN EXPERT, AI ENGINEER, BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT, NEXUS3 ECOSYSTEM, WHY THIS MODEL WORKS, LEGAL: SPECIALTY & MID-MARKET, HEALTHCARE REVENUE CYCLE, INSURANCE
- **Images:** 4 total, all 4 with alt text = **100% alt coverage** (good) — but all 4 are inline base64-encoded PNGs rather than optimized external image files.
- **Image formats:** 100% PNG (base64 data URIs), 0% WebP/JPEG/SVG as external assets.
- **Lazy loading:** 0 of 4 images (0%) use `loading="lazy"` — low impact here given there are only 4 images, but worth adopting as a habit.
- **Robots meta tag:** `index,follow,max-image-preview:large,max-snippet:-1,max-video-preview:-1` — correctly configured, fully indexable.
- **Viewport:** `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0` — correct.

## Conclusion

nexus3.ai is a well-built, security-conscious site (100/100 Best Practices, clean robots.txt, fast desktop load times) let down by underinvested SEO fundamentals — a 6-character title tag, no og:image, no structured data, and nine H1 tags on a single page. Mobile performance also needs attention: the PSI lab run shows FCP/LCP in "Poor" territory under throttled conditions, a sharp contrast to WebPageTest's sub-1.1s desktop numbers. None of these are hard problems — title/meta/schema fixes are same-day work, and the heading hierarchy is a straightforward cleanup. **Overall grade: B-** — strong technical foundation, but the metadata and heading-structure gaps are actively costing this site visibility in search.
