## Gap Analysis: What Achieve PT Should Act On

Ranked by impact potential:

1. **Activate "discovery call" in body copy** — The CTA exists in metadata. Smith PT+ proves this framing converts (they built their entire lead gen around a near-identical phrase). Adding it to homepage, services pages, and treatment pages costs nothing and directly closes the gap with the most conversion-optimized competitor.

2. **Fix title truncation (Squarespace suffix)** — 13/22 pages truncated. Loop PT is worse (20/25), Crystal Lake PT is better (3/25). A Squarespace theme-level fix would make Achieve PT the title-tag leader in this market.

3. **Implement schema markup** — Zero competitors have functioning schema. First mover gets rich results (star ratings, business info, practitioner cards) uncontested.

4. **Add pricing transparency or financing** — Crystal Lake PT is the only clinic removing price anxiety. Even a simple "we accept these insurances" page or a "what to expect" pricing overview would differentiate from Loop PT, Team Rehab, and Smith PT+.

5. **Expand women''s health blog content** — Crystal Lake PT owns this content lane (10+ posts on pelvic floor, postpartum, incontinence, mastectomy). Achieve PT has the same positioning but half the content depth.
