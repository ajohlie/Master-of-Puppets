## 7. Actionable Recommendations

1. **Lead with a free offer** — Free screening or assessment is table stakes in this market.
2. **Show your team** — Named therapists with credentials convert. Advocate and DPT don't do this.
3. **Target specialty keywords** — "Dry needling Barrington IL", "pelvic floor therapy Barrington", "vestibular therapy Barrington" — all have low competition.
4. **Saturday availability** — If you have it, lead with it. Only Athletico offers it.
5. **Out-content Advocate** — Their page is basically empty. Any page with real service descriptions will outrank it.
6. **Insurance list** — Publish a full accepted insurance list. Patients search this before calling.
7. **MindBody/holistic angle** — DPT owns "MindBody PT" but doesn't push it hard. Room to go deeper.
