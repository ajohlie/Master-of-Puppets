## Finalist Candidates (7)

### 1. Loop Physical Therapy
- **URL:** https://looppt.com
- **Location:** Barrington, IL + Chicago locations (Edgewater, The Loop, West Loop, Terrace Clinic)
- **Distance from Barrington:** 0 miles (has Barrington clinic)
- **Why relevant:** Direct same-market competitor. Explicitly markets pelvic floor therapy AND women's health therapy to Barrington, IL patients by name. Multi-location practice with a specialty PT anchor. Surfaced in both the pelvic floor query and the women's health query.
- **Surfaced by queries:** #4 (pelvic floor Barrington), #7 (women's health Chicago suburbs)
- **Snippet positioning:** "Our expert physical therapists…Edgewater, The Chicago Loop, West Loop Chicago, IL, Terrace Clinic and **Barrington, IL** physical therapists" — they list Barrington alongside their Chicago locations, suggesting it's a growth market for them.

### 2. Crystal Lake Physical Therapy
- **URL:** https://www.crystallakept.com
- **Location:** 735 McArdle Dr., Unit A, Crystal Lake, IL 60014
- **Distance from Barrington:** ~18 miles north
- **Why relevant:** Strongest specialty alignment in the set. Self-describes as "A Women's and Men's Health Outpatient Orthopedic Physical Therapy Clinic." Treats pelvic pain, incontinence, postpartum, post-mastectomy. Uses 1-on-1 licensed PT model (no aides) — identical to Achieve's positioning. Voted Best PT Clinic in McHenry County 2023 and 2024. Blog posts: "One Postpartum Visit Is Not Enough!", "Incontinence is NOT a normal part of aging!"
- **Surfaced by queries:** #2 (Lake County IL), #5 (Crystal Lake IL)
- **Snippet positioning:** "You will only be seen by a licensed physical therapist…full undivided attention. Because we don't use unlicensed support staff to provide your treatment we believe that you will get better faster." Nearly identical differentiation language to Achieve PT.

### 3. Team Rehabilitation — Lake Barrington
- **URL:** https://team-rehab.com/location/lake-barrington/
- **Location:** Lake Barrington, IL (adjacent to Barrington, ~2 miles)
- **Distance from Barrington:** ~2 miles
